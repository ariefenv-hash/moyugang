## Description:

Turns a selected game concept into a playable design covering the player promise, core loop, world rules, level pacing, feedback, failure, and a prototype slice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[worldwonderer](https://clawhub.ai/user/worldwonderer)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Game designers and developers use this skill to turn an approved concept and product brief into a scoped game design document, including systems, levels, and narrative branches when relevant.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill writes a design document into the project.

Mitigation: Confirm the target project and review the proposed design document before adopting it.

Risk: Chinese-language design references may be difficult for English-only reviewers to assess.

Mitigation: Arrange a bilingual review of the design guidance and resulting document when needed.
