## Style Tokens

These are the positive aesthetic signals that define this taste:

- `calligraphic line as form` — every stroke is simultaneously writing and drawing
- `ink wash gradation` — the full spectrum from concentrated black to barely-there grey
- `negative space as composition` — emptiness that structures rather than merely surrounds
- `colophon as creative layer` — inscriptions that extend the work, not explain it
- `spontaneous brushwork` — controlled spontaneity that reveals character
- `scholarly restraint` — what is withheld matters as much as what is shown
- `bamboo-bone structure` — internal structural logic borrowed from calligraphic anatomy
- `wet-dry ink contrast` — breathing rhythm in the brush
- `mounted scroll format` — the physical form shapes the viewing experience
- `poetic inscription integration` — text and image as one creative act

## Anti-Tokens

These signals are incompatible with this aesthetic:

- `photorealistic rendering`
- `saturated color fields`
- `geometric precision`
- `digital smoothness`
- `decorative excess`
- `narrative illustration`

## Application Rules

1. Every stroke must function simultaneously as writing and image — if it works only as one, it fails as literati art
2. Negative space is not emptiness but active compositional breathing — it must feel intentional, not accidental
3. The colophon (題跋) is not caption but continuation — it extends the work rather than explaining it
4. Restraint over display: the brush reveals character through what it withholds, not what it shows off
5. Wet-dry ink transitions must feel like natural breathing rhythm, not mechanical alternation

## Evaluation Criteria

- Does each brushstroke carry both calligraphic energy and pictorial meaning?
- Is the negative space active and compositionally necessary, or merely unfilled?
- Does the inscription feel like it belongs to the same creative act as the image?
- Would removing any element make the whole feel incomplete?
- Does the work reveal scholarly restraint rather than technical showmanship?
